VocaLock — Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026 · LearnoBay

Prices: ₹1 for the first day, then ₹199 every 30 days until you cancel.

Cancelling

Cancel at any time in the app: Profile → Payment Settings → Cancel subscription. The mandate is stopped straight away and nothing further is charged. You keep Premium until the end of the period you have already paid for. Revoking the AutoPay mandate from your UPI app also cancels it.

Trial

The ₹1 trial charge is not refundable — it is a nominal amount taken to set up the payment mandate.

Renewal charges

If you were charged after cancelling, or charged twice, write to us within 7 days and we will refund it in full. Refunds reach your account in 5 to 7 working days.

Change of mind

We do not refund part-used 30-day periods. Cancel before your renewal date and you will not be charged again.

How to ask

Email support@indidino.com with the date and amount, and the UPI reference from your payment app.
