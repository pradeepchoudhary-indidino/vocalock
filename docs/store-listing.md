## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Lost your phone somewhere in the house? Just clap. VocaLock hears it and makes
your phone ring, flash and vibrate — even on silent.

Want to put your phone down and actually stay off it? Say your phrase and the
screen locks.

Everything happens on your phone. No audio is ever uploaded, and VocaLock works
with no internet at all.


CLAP TO FIND

• Clap and your phone rings, even when it is on silent
• The flashlight strobes so you can spot it in the dark
• Adjustable sensitivity, and you choose how many claps it takes
• Whistle detection, if you would rather whistle than clap
• Ignores sound coming from your own phone, so a clap in a video will not set
  it off


VOICE LOCK

• Record a phrase. Say it, and your screen locks.
• Choose how it locks: use your phone's own lock screen, or have VocaLock cover
  the screen and lift it with a second spoken phrase
• A backup PIN always gets you back in
• Works in English and Hindi


PRIVACY

VocaLock listens through the microphone — that is the whole point of it — so we
are exact about what happens to that sound:

• Audio is processed on the device, in memory, and discarded immediately
• Nothing is recorded to a file. Nothing is uploaded. Ever.
• The speech model is bundled inside the app and runs offline
• Your backup PIN is never stored, only a one-way hash of it


PLEASE READ

VocaLock is a convenience and focus tool, not a security product.

If you choose "cover my screen", that cover can be got past by anyone who
force-stops the app in Android settings. If you choose "use my phone's lock",
your phone is genuinely locked by Android and your usual fingerprint or PIN
unlocks it.

Clap and voice detection depend on background noise and will not work every
single time. Some phones with aggressive battery management may stop the
listener.


SUBSCRIPTION

Premium is ₹1 for the first 3 days, then ₹499 per month until cancelled. Cancel
any time from Profile → Payment Settings.


Questions: support@indidino.com
```
