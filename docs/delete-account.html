Delete your VocaLock account and data

Indidino · app ID com.indidino.vocalock

VocaLock stores almost nothing. Your phrases, settings and backup PIN never leave your phone. The only thing held about you is the mobile number you signed in with, and the status of your subscription.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open VocaLock
  2. Tap the profile icon, top right
  3. Scroll down and tap Sign out
  4. Confirm

That removes your number, your session and your plan from the device immediately. Uninstalling VocaLock removes everything else it has stored, including your phrases and your backup PIN hash.

Ask us to delete it

To have the account removed from our records as well, email support@indidino.com from any address, with the mobile number you signed in with, and the subject Delete my VocaLock account.

We action deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by reply when it is done.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your mobile number, your account identifier, and your subscription record.
  • Never held in the first place: any audio. It is processed on your phone and discarded, so there is nothing to delete.
  • Kept: payment records we are required to retain for tax and accounting purposes, for as long as the law requires. These are not used for anything else.
