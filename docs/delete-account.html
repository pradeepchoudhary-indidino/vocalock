Delete your VocaLock account and data

LearnoBay · app ID com.indidino.vocalock

VocaLock stores very little about you. Your phrases, settings and backup PIN never leave your phone. Our server holds only your Google name and email, Google's identifier for your account, and the record of your subscription and its payments.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open VocaLock
  2. Tap the profile icon, top right
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account
  4. Tap Delete my account to confirm

This happens immediately, and you are signed out on every phone. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. To stop payments, cancel it in the app under Profile → Payment Settings (or in your UPI app) before or after deleting. Uninstalling VocaLock then removes what it stored on the phone itself, including your phrases and your backup PIN hash.

No longer have the app?

Email support@indidino.com from the Google email address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my VocaLock account. We act on deletion requests within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleting in the app: closes your account. Your name, email, Google account identifier, device details and account records are kept, and signing in again with the same Google account restores your account.
  • To have your data erased: email support@indidino.com from the Google email address you signed in with. We erase your name, email, Google account identifier and device details within 30 days and reply when it is done.
  • Always: every sign-in session ends.
  • Not affected: your subscription. It continues, and is charged, until you cancel it.
  • Never held in the first place: any audio, and your card or UPI details (those stay with Razorpay).
  • Kept: the record of payments you made, as tax law requires. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for 90 days and carry only an internal account number.
